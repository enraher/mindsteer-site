Millionaire Trivia · Privacy policy

25 November 2026

Who runs the game

Millionaire Trivia for Android (com.enrasoft.millionaire.quiz) is provided by Enrique Ramírez Hernández (Enrasoft), Spain. For privacy questions and rights requests, write to enraher@gmail.com.

Playing and saving progress

The core quiz runs offline. Your device stores the selected question language and categories, your settings, scores, question history and brain balance. The game does not offer an Enrasoft account or cloud synchronization. Uninstalling or clearing app data can delete the local progress and balance.

Optional purchases

Google Play processes purchases and manages payment details and order history. The app receives purchase details and the signed purchase token to verify the purchase and grant the items or ad-removal benefit. Version 1.2.3 stores a local hash of the token to prevent double credit. The app does not receive full card details. Google handles its payment records under its own privacy policy.

Ads and privacy choices

Google Mobile Ads / AdMob supplies banners, interstitials and optional rewarded videos. The SDK may collect or share IP addresses, approximate location derived from the IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices. The lock icon in the menu lets you return to the ad privacy form when it is available.

Google Play Games and analytics

If you use Google Play Games, Google can process your player profile and identifier, game activity and leaderboard scores. Google controls the visibility and privacy settings of that profile. Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in version 1.2.3. Earlier versions may send usage events and technical information to Firebase Analytics depending on their settings.

Support, providers and your rights

If you contact us, we use your email and the information you provide to answer and handle the request. Google and the host of this policy can process connection data outside your country under their own terms. Local game data stays on the device until removed. Google sets retention periods for its service records. You can manage or delete Google Play Games data in your Google settings. For access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or other applicable data rights, email the address above. If applicable, you can also contact your local data protection authority.
