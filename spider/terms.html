The experience

Spider / Araña displays virtual creatures using the camera. Bites are simulated and skin contact is approximate. You need a compatible device, camera permission, adequate lighting and gentle movement. The experience is not recorded automatically.

Free sessions

The hand spider includes one free 15-second session each day. Time continues if you remove your hand or move the app to the background. When ads are available, rewarded ads can earn up to four extra sessions per day. Closing without earning a reward does not grant a session. The daily limit resets at 00:00 UTC. Network, consent and advertising availability may prevent extra sessions.

Premium

Premium includes unlimited sessions, no ads, available catalogue creatures and their use on hand and face. Monthly and annual plans are offered. Your store shows local pricing, period, applicable taxes and any eligible trial before confirmation. Trial eligibility is determined by the store and your account.

Renewal, cancellation and restoration

After a trial, the subscription renews at the price and period in your store confirmation unless cancelled in time. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Restore compatible store purchases through the app. Refunds are handled by the relevant store without limiting applicable statutory rights.
