Help

Contact support@mindsteer.app and mention Spider / Araña, app version and device model. Privacy and deletion: privacy@mindsteer.app. Do not send passwords, bank details or images of other people.

Camera

Allow camera access in device settings. Use good lighting and show one hand or face. Move the device and hand gently.

Subscriptions

Use Restore purchases to check access. Manage or cancel in Apple or Google subscriptions. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.
