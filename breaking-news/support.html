How do I join the beta?

The first Google Play internal test and TestFlight build are in preparation. Installation links will appear here when available.

Do I need an account?

You can read and save headlines without signing up. An optional account is intended for synchronizing reading preferences and saved items.

Does Premium unlock publisher articles?

No. Articles open on the publisher’s website. Any publisher subscription or paywall is separate.

How do I manage a subscription?

Use your Apple or Google Play subscription settings. Removing the app or deleting its account does not cancel a subscription.

Why did a source or alert stop updating?

Check connectivity and notification permissions. Source availability and free backend quotas can also affect updates. Contact support with your app version and device model; do not send passwords or purchase credentials.
