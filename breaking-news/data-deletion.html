Delete account

Updated October 7, 2026

To request deletion of your Breaking News account and associated synchronized data, email privacy@mindsteer.app with the subject “Breaking News account deletion”.

What to include

Write from the email associated with your Breaking News account and state that you want the account and its synchronized reading data deleted. If you use Apple’s private email relay or cannot access that email, explain this so we can verify ownership another way. Do not send your password, identity documents or payment credentials.

What the request covers

The request covers the Firebase authentication account, synchronized preferences and saved headlines, and associated push registrations. Information that must be retained for legal or security reasons, and infrastructure backups awaiting their normal expiry, may require limited retention. We will explain any applicable exception. You can remove local saved items and reset reading preferences in the app’s Settings; clear device app data to remove its local cache.

Store subscriptions are separate

Deleting an account or the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel active subscriptions in Apple or Google Play settings and request any refund through that store.
