Privacy

Updated October 7, 2026

This policy explains how Breaking News handles information when you read, sign in, save headlines or enable alerts.

Who is responsible

Breaking News is provided by ENRASOFT APPS SL, based in Spain. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email privacy@mindsteer.app and mention Breaking News.

Information used by the app

Without signing in, reading preferences and saved headlines are stored on your device. News requests transmit selected source identifiers. Enabling push sends an installation identifier, a push delivery token, your country, app language, selected sources and topics, quiet-hour settings and any personal alert rules to our backend. If you sign in, Firebase processes your account email, user ID and provider identifiers; your reading preferences and saved headlines can be associated with that account for synchronization. Subscription services process store transaction and entitlement information. We do not receive your card number.

Purpose and legal basis

We use account and reading information to provide the features you request, based on performance of the service contract. Optional notifications and advertising storage or processing that requires consent use the applicable permission or consent. We use limited technical information to operate and secure the service based on legitimate interests, and retain information required by law where applicable. You can read without creating an account, disable alerts and change available advertising privacy choices.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google and Apple process sign-in when you choose their buttons. Cloudflare provides the news API and database. RevenueCat and Apple or Google Play manage subscription verification. When ads are displayed, Google AdMob may process device and advertising information subject to your choices and applicable consent. Links to publishers open their websites, which have their own privacy notices.

Advertising and the beta

The initial beta uses official test ads and store sandbox purchases. Test integrations still communicate with their providers. Production advertising and paid subscriptions will be enabled separately. Non-personalized ads can still involve device information and local storage; available privacy choices appear in the app when required. Premium removes in-app ads.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable, and withdraw consent for consent-based processing. Send requests to privacy@mindsteer.app. We may ask you to confirm account ownership without requesting your password. You may complain to a data protection authority, including the AEPD in Spain. The account and data deletion page explains how to request deletion. Deleting an account does not cancel a store subscription.

Retention, security and transfers

Local saved headlines remain until you remove them or clear app data. Account information and synchronized preferences remain while needed to provide your account, or until a valid deletion request is completed, subject to legal and security retention requirements. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs and backups under their own retention policies. We use HTTPS and access controls. Providers may process information internationally; their applicable data processing agreements and transfer mechanisms govern those transfers. A database location hint does not guarantee that all processing stays in that region.

Children and updates

This service is not directed to children under 16. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We update this page when the service or its processing changes.
