Privacy

Updated October 13, 2026

This policy explains how Breaking News handles information when you read, sign in, save headlines or enable alerts.

Who is responsible

The data controller for Breaking News / Noticias de última hora is Enrique Ramírez Hernández, Spanish tax ID 70519665D. Address: Calle Roca, 46007 Valencia, Spain. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email privacy@mindsteer.app and mention Breaking News.

Information used by the app

Without signing in, reading preferences, saved headlines and private notes are stored on your device. Headlines and publisher photos download directly from publishers, who receive normal network information such as your IP address. Enabling push sends an installation identifier, a push delivery token, your country, app language, selected sources and topics, quiet-hour settings and any personal alert rules to our backend. If you sign in, Firebase processes your account email, user ID and provider identifiers; your reading preferences, saved headlines and private notes can be associated with that account for synchronization. Subscription services process store transaction and entitlement information. We do not receive your card number.

Purpose and legal basis

We use account and reading information to provide the features you request, based on performance of the service contract. Optional notifications and advertising storage or processing that requires consent use the applicable permission or consent. We use limited technical information to operate and secure the service based on legitimate interests, and retain information required by law where applicable. You can read without creating an account, disable alerts and change available advertising privacy choices.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google, Apple and Meta process sign-in when you choose their buttons. Facebook Login is optional and requests only your public profile and email address when available. Firebase Authentication receives the Facebook credential to create or access your account. On iOS, Facebook uses Limited Login with automatic app event logging and advertising identifier collection disabled. Cloudflare provides account synchronization, Premium verification, notification services and their database. RevenueCat and Apple or Google Play manage subscription verification. When ads are displayed, Google AdMob may process device and advertising information subject to your choices and applicable consent. Links to publishers open their websites, which have their own privacy notices.

Advertising and the beta

The initial beta uses official test ads and store sandbox purchases. Test integrations still communicate with their providers. Production advertising and paid subscriptions will be enabled separately. Non-personalized ads can still involve device information and local storage; available privacy choices appear in the app when required. Premium removes in-app ads.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable, and withdraw consent for consent-based processing. Send requests to privacy@mindsteer.app. We may ask you to confirm account ownership without requesting your password. You may complain to a data protection authority, including the AEPD in Spain. The account and data deletion page explains how to request deletion. Deleting an account does not cancel a store subscription.

Retention, security and transfers

The news feed cache retains stories for seven days, with up to 50 per publisher. Saved headlines and private notes remain until you remove them or clear app data. Account information and synchronized preferences remain while needed to provide your account, or until a valid deletion request is completed, subject to legal and security retention requirements. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs and backups under their own retention policies. We use HTTPS and access controls. Providers may process information internationally; their applicable data processing agreements and transfer mechanisms govern those transfers. A database location hint does not guarantee that all processing stays in that region.

Presentation website and publisher browsing

This presentation website includes no analytics or advertising scripts and sets no first-party cookies. Its hosting provider may process technical connection data to deliver and secure it. Publisher websites are third-party services: when you download their feeds or images or open a story in the in-app or external browser, they may receive your IP address, browser and device information and any information you give them. Their pages may use cookies, advertising and analytics under their own policies and consent choices. Advertising consent in our app does not replace consent on those websites; Premium does not remove their advertising.

Preferences, places and advertising choices

The sources, topics and places you select help organize your news and alerts. Selected places are preferences you provide to the service. Alert rules apply automatically and you can change or disable them. Where available, review advertising privacy options in app settings and manage device permissions. Refusing optional processing does not prevent reading without an account; it may affect the feature that needs that permission.

Support and rights complaints

If you contact support or submit a complaint, we process your contact details, message and necessary evidence to respond, verify the request and manage the issue. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in supporting and protecting the service and, where applicable, compliance with legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. We retain this information as needed to handle the case and for applicable legal periods. Provide only necessary information; do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Children and updates

This service is not directed to children under 16. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We update this page when the service or its processing changes.
