Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Controller and scope

Enrique Ramírez Hernández (Enrasoft), Spain, is the controller for 6 of 6 for Android, package com.enrasoft.questions.quiz.pro. For privacy questions and rights requests, contact enrasoft@gmail.com. This policy covers this game and enquiries you send about it.

Game data on your device

The game stores question progress, scores, brains, settings, language and purchase-credit markers on your device. These data let you continue playing, use hints and avoid crediting a purchase twice. We do not provide our own game-account server or a wallet synchronised between devices. Android cloud backup is disabled in the app configuration; clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local progress and balances.

Purchases and optional rewards

Google Play handles checkout, payment methods and its transaction history. The game receives product and purchase state, quantity, signatures and purchase-token information to verify transactions and deliver the corresponding brains or benefits. It stores local credit markers, including token hashes, to prevent duplicate grants. The app does not collect your bank-card details. Rewarded videos are optional and grant the reward after the advertising SDK reports completion. Google processes purchases and payments under its own terms and privacy policy.

Advertising and consent

Google Mobile Ads / AdMob displays banners, interstitials at round boundaries and optional rewarded videos. Its SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This processing is not made anonymous merely because an SDK sends the information. Advertising personalisation and the available choices depend on your consent, region, account and device. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents a consent form. Advertising privacy choices remain accessible from the app’s Privacy options.

Optional analytics and crash diagnostics

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are included for usage statistics and fault diagnosis. Optional collection is disabled initially and is governed by the choices stored on the device, including choices preserved from earlier versions. When enabled, these services can process app-instance identifiers, events and interactions, technical device/app information and crash reports to understand usage and resolve problems. Provider retention and processing settings apply; these data are not automatically ephemeral.

Google Play Games

Google Play Games services can use your Google player profile, player identifier and scores for sign-in and leaderboard features. These are external Google-account features, not an Enrasoft game account. Manage your profile and related privacy choices through Google Play Games and your Google account.

More games / More apps catalogue

Opening the catalogue requests the public portfolio feed from mindsteer.app, hosted on GitHub Pages. This request does not include your brains, progress, installed-app inventory or game-account details. GitHub receives normal technical connection information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. App icons are requested from Google image hosts. Opening a card takes you to Google Play, whose availability and data practices depend on your country, device and account.

Permissions and communications

An internet connection is used for ads, consent, purchases, Play Games and the catalogue. The trivia game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access to play. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to include and use them to answer your request. Do not send passwords, full payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Purposes, providers and international processing

We use necessary game and purchase data to deliver the requested service and prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions. Consent governs optional processing where required; security and support may also rely on legitimate interests or legal duties, as applicable. Google provides the advertising, consent, payment, games and optional analytics/diagnostic services; GitHub provides the public website and catalogue hosting. These providers may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA, under the safeguards and terms described in their policies.

Retention, choices and your rights

Local game data remain until removed through app/device controls or uninstalling. Google and GitHub manage their service data under their respective retention settings and policies; we do not promise that deleting local progress erases Google’s purchase records. You can revisit advertising choices in Privacy options, manage your Google-account controls, and contact us to request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or objection where applicable. You may withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing and complain to your data-protection authority, including the Spanish AEPD. We may request only information necessary to identify and handle the request.

Children and changes

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s information, contact us so we can assess the request and the services involved. Device, store and Google-account controls may provide additional safeguards. We will update this page when the app’s data practices change; the date above identifies this version.
