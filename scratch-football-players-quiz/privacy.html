Scratch Football Player Quiz

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Controller and scope

Enrique Ramírez Hernández (Enrasoft), Spain, is the controller for Scratch Football Player Quiz for Android, package com.enrasoft.scratch.football.players.quiz.v2017. For privacy questions or rights requests, contact enrasoft@gmail.com. This policy covers the renewed Android version 3.0.0 (22) and later versions with the same data practices, and enquiries you send us about it. It does not describe the advertising services used by older, withdrawn versions.

Game progress and local data

You do not need to create a game account. Progress, answers, scores, coins, settings and markers for purchases already credited are stored on your device to continue games and prevent duplicate grants. This version disables automatic Android backup for app data. The age band is also stored in a file excluded from backups. We do not provide progress or coin synchronisation between devices. When updating from an earlier version, game progress, coins and difficulty start over and are not imported.

Google Play purchases

Google Play manages the checkout form, payment method and transaction history. Through Play Billing, the game receives the product, purchase state, quantity and verification data to validate the purchase, grant coins and consume the product when appropriate. It retains a hash of the purchase token locally as a credit marker. The game does not provide its own bank-card entry form. Google processes payment data under its own terms and privacy policy.

The Special Pack is a one-time, non-consumable purchase: it includes 700 coins once, removes banners and interstitials and unlocks Easy and Hard. Rewarded videos remain optional. Ownership is checked with Google Play and permanent benefits can be restored using the same Play account. Restoring an already acknowledged purchase does not grant the 700 coins again. The balance and progress remain local; we do not provide a wallet synchronised across devices.

More apps catalogue

When you open the unified More apps catalogue, the app requests a public list of our published apps from mindsteer.app, hosted on GitHub Pages. The list is refreshed from public Google Play developer pages. This catalogue request does not include your game progress, coins, installed-app inventory or game-specific identifiers. GitHub receives technical connection data, including IP addresses, and stores IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy policy. App icons are loaded from Google image services; selecting an app opens Google Play or its website. No separate catalogue account is created.

Optional local notifications

Notifications are off until you accept them; Android versions that require it also ask for system permission. These are game reminders scheduled on your device using text included in the app: there is no dedicated push server or subscription to remote news or update announcements. Scheduling allows up to two daytime reminders per day between 10:00 and 21:00, spaced apart; Android may delay or prevent delivery.

Your choice and markers needed to prevent duplicate reminders are stored locally. You can turn notifications off in game Settings or Android notification settings. Turning them off in the game cancels pending alarms and notifications already displayed. This choice is independent of advertising consent and optional usage statistics and crash reports.

Advertising and consent

We use Google AdMob for banners, ads at breaks between rounds and rewarded videos you choose to watch. These services may receive IP addresses —which can indicate approximate location—, device or installation identifiers, interactions and technical performance data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalised ads may also use technical data and identifiers depending on the applicable configuration.

Google User Messaging Platform manages advertising choices and consent where relevant to your region. We request ads when the platform permits it. You can reopen available choices through Settings → Privacy options. You can also manage the advertising ID through Android settings. These choices do not replace the consent options of external websites you choose to visit.

Usage statistics and error reports

The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand use and diagnose faults. They may process usage events, technical app and device information, installation identifiers and error details. Supporting Firebase services may process installation identifiers and metadata necessary to operate.

Statistics collection and automatic crash-report submission are off by default in this version. An update may retain earlier preferences. Change them independently through Settings → Privacy options → Usage statistics or Error reports. The controls update the corresponding SDK collection setting when changed; reports already queued or sent are subject to the provider’s handling. Crashlytics may store crashes locally while submission is disabled and send them if you enable it later. Turning an option off does not delete data already sent.

Purposes, legal grounds and recipients

We process data necessary to provide the game and handle purchases; optional preferences depend on your choice and, where applicable, consent. We may also process information to answer enquiries, prevent abuse, protect the service or meet legal obligations under the applicable legal ground. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Google provides the advertising, consent, billing and Firebase services described above; GitHub hosts the public catalogue and this policy. It may act as a service provider or as controller for its own processing, depending on the product. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to address a valid request. You do not need to send passwords or bank details for a privacy enquiry.

Retention, security and international transfers

Local data remains until removed or no longer needed to maintain progress and credited purchases. Google retains technical, advertising or transaction data according to the service, its configuration and its obligations. Enquiries are kept as needed to resolve them and for applicable periods needed to meet obligations or handle claims.

The Google SDKs described above encrypt data in transit. Their services may process information outside your country, including the United States and other places where their providers operate, using the applicable transfer mechanisms described in their policies. Local storage does not mean advertising or diagnostic services receive no information.

Your choices and rights

You can clear app data through Android. Clearing data or uninstalling may remove local progress and coins. Restoring permanent purchase benefits through Google Play does not recover consumed coins or previous game progress. This does not automatically erase Google Play history or data already received by other providers.

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability, or withdraw consent, by emailing enrasoft@gmail.com. State the app and your request without unnecessary sensitive information. We may ask for proportionate information to identify the request or explain which provider should address it. There is no game account to close. You may complain to your competent supervisory authority, including the Spanish Data Protection Agency in Spain.

Minors, external links and this website

Before advertising consent is requested, it asks for an age band, including a prefer-not-to-say option, without asking for a birth date. The age band is stored locally outside Android backups; it is not sent as an Analytics user property. Google receives the corresponding age-treatment settings for advertising. Users under 16 and users who do not state an age receive restricted advertising treatment, without a personalised-ad consent request; optional usage statistics and error-report submission are disabled. Users aged 16–17 receive teen advertising treatment. Adults receive the regional advertising consent flow. You can change the age band in Settings → Privacy options. If you are a minor, use the game with any authorisation required by applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child has improperly provided data so we can investigate and address the request.

The game may open external services such as Google Play or its community. Those services apply their own policies. This page contains no advertising or analytics scripts; GitHub Pages may process technical connection data to host and protect it. See the policies of GitHub, Google and Firebase.

Changes

We update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The date on this page identifies the current version. This policy does not change the terms of other Enrasoft apps.
