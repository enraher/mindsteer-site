Privacy policy

Published · 2026-11-21

This policy applies to AR Ghosts Radar, an augmented reality game featuring fictional apparitions. The app is intended for people aged 13 and older. The data controller is Enrique Ramírez Hernández (Enrasoft Apps). Privacy contact privacy@mindsteer.app.

Camera and captures. The camera places virtual apparitions in your surroundings. The app processes camera images on your device without sending them to Enrasoft, Google Ads or RevenueCat. If you choose to take a photo or record, the app prepares the capture on your device and saves it to Photos or your gallery if permitted. The video contains the scene image and does not request microphone access. The app removes the temporary file when saving finishes. If interrupted, you can remove leftover temporary files by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. Gallery copies are managed separately.

Age and preferences. Your age band, language, settings and free session usage are stored on your device. We do not ask for your date of birth or create a user account. We send Google age treatment signals to configure ads. Advertising is restricted for teenagers, and the app does not ask them to authorize tracking on iOS.

Purchases and restoration. Apple or Google processes payments. RevenueCat uses an installation identifier, purchase details, purchase status and technical information such as app version, operating system and storefront or country to verify and restore access. The app does not receive card details. Purchase information is used to provide the service and resolve support requests and is kept as long as necessary for those purposes and applicable obligations.

Advertising and your choices. The free version can offer optional ads to extend a session. AdMob and its providers may process IP addresses, permitted identifiers, ad interactions and technical or diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and detect fraud. An IP address may reveal approximate location. Ghost Radar does not request GPS access. Options depend on your region and device. You can review consent in Settings when available and change your tracking permission in iPhone settings. The initial free session remains available when ads are unavailable. Premium and the previous no-ads purchase do not show ads.

Recipients and your rights. See the privacy policies of Google, Apple and RevenueCat. Providers may process data outside your country. Contact privacy support for details about retention and transfers or to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable. You can withdraw consent for processing based on consent and complain to your data protection authority. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
