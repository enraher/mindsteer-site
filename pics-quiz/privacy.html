PICS Quiz

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

PICS Quiz 2.0 for Android · the.pics.quiz.guess.trivia

Controller and scope

Enrique Ramírez Hernández (Enrasoft), Spain, is responsible for PICS Quiz. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact enrasoft@gmail.com. This policy describes the renewed Android version 2.0 and enquiries you send us about it. Earlier versions may have different features and choices.

Games and device data

You do not need a separate PICS account to play. We store progress, unfinished answers, scores, brains, unlocked levels, hints and settings on your device so you can continue playing. We also store tokens for purchases already granted to prevent duplicate delivery. We do not upload this game profile to our database or provide our own cross-device game synchronization.

The app allows Android backups. The system may back up or restore data depending on your device, account and settings. Local saving does not exclude those backups or the external services described below.

Downloading the catalogue and pictures

We use Firebase Realtime Database and Cloud Storage to check versions and download the catalogue, names and pictures. Requests provide the service with connection information, such as an IP address and technical metadata needed to deliver and protect the service. These downloads do not upload your answers or game progress. Supporting Firebase services may also use installation identifiers and technical data.

More games catalogue

When you open More games, we download a public catalogue of our apps from mindsteer.app, hosted on GitHub Pages. The list is updated outside the app; PICS itself is hidden and available titles are selected for your device language locally. We do not send your brains, answers, progress, purchases or accounts to query this catalogue.

HTTPS requests provide the hosting service with your IP address and ordinary technical connection data. Other apps’ icons are downloaded from Google image servers. Tapping a listing opens Google Play or its web page if the native store is unavailable; Google determines availability for your country and device.

Advertising and your choices

Google AdMob provides banners, ads during round breaks and rewarded videos you choose to watch. It may process IP address, approximate location inferred from it, identifiers, interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads may also use technical data.

Google User Messaging Platform manages ad choices and consent where applicable. We request ads when that platform permits it. You can return to the available choices through Settings → Privacy options → Ad privacy choices. You can also manage your advertising identifier in Android settings. Available choices depend on your region and the service.

Optional analytics and crash reports

Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics help us understand usage and diagnose faults. They may process gameplay events, app and device metadata, installation identifiers and technical error details. Both are disabled by default for new installations; an update preserves your previous choices.

You can change them separately in Settings → Privacy options. Turning off Usage analytics stops that collection and resets local analytics data; it does not erase server history. Turning off Crash reports takes full effect on the next app start. Crashlytics may temporarily store reports on the device; this version requests deletion of pending reports without sending them when the option is off. Disabling an option does not delete data Google has already received.

These switches do not change your progress or ad preferences. They do not disable processing by AdMob, Google Play or Play Games, or the data needed to download content.

Leaderboards with Google Play Games

Play Games may automatically authenticate an existing player profile. The app avoids asking you to create a profile at startup; you can start that process by opening Leaderboard. That screen submits your total score to Google’s ranking. The SDK may process player identity, diagnostics and usage data under Google’s settings.

Your Play Games preferences control profile and score visibility. You can manage or delete game data through your Play Games profile or Google account. PICS does not use this service to synchronize local game progress or request your friends list.

Purchases through Google Play

Google Play handles payment and transaction history. Through Play Billing, PICS receives the product identifier, purchase state and token to grant brains and the no-ads benefit where applicable, record delivery and consume the product. The token is retained as a local receipt marker. We do not provide our own card-entry form or receive your card number. Google processes payment under its own terms and policy.

Enquiries and reports you choose to send

Reporting a picture prepares an email containing the topic, level, picture, language, problem type and your comment. Your email app displays it and you choose whether to send it. We do not automatically send the comment through Firebase. If you contact us, we receive your address and the contents you include to handle your enquiry. Do not attach passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Purposes, legal bases and recipients

We process data needed to provide the game and grant purchases as part of the service you request. Optional features depend on your choice and, where applicable, your consent. We may also process information under legitimate interests to handle enquiries and protect the service, or to meet legal obligations. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Google provides the services listed and may act as a service provider or as controller for its own processing, depending on the product. Your email provider is involved if you send an enquiry. We may disclose information where required by law or to address a valid request. Game progress is not used to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Retention, security and transfers

Local game data and receipt markers remain for as long as needed to maintain progress and granted purchases, or until you delete them. Android backups depend on system settings. We retain enquiries as needed to resolve them and for applicable legal or claims periods. Google data is retained according to the service, its settings and obligations; consult the linked policies or contact us about a specific request.

We use encrypted connections for downloads and the Google services described. No measure guarantees absolute security. Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, using the applicable mechanisms described in their policies and terms. Google’s information on data transfers explains those mechanisms.

Your choices and rights

You can manage app data and backups through Android. Clearing data or uninstalling may lose local progress and brains; a system restore may recover backed-up data. This does not automatically erase Google Play history, Play Games scores or data other providers have already received.

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability, or withdraw consent, by writing to enrasoft@gmail.com. Identify PICS Quiz and your request. We may ask for proportionate information to identify the request and guide you if a provider needs to handle it. There is no separate PICS account to close. You may complain to your data protection authority; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Minors, external services and this website

If you are a minor, use the game with any authorization required by applicable law. If you believe a minor has improperly provided information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address the request.

External services you choose to open have their own policies. This page contains no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages may process technical connection data to host and protect it. Links to Google, Firebase and GitHub policies appear below.

Changes

We will update this policy if PICS or its processing changes. The page date identifies this version. This policy is specific to PICS Quiz and does not replace policies for other Enrasoft apps.
