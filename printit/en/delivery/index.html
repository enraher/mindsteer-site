Delivery

The shop shows the available destinations. Enter your address to check whether we can deliver your items, how much shipping costs and the estimated dates before you pay. If your order is split into several shipments, you can track each one in the app or using the link in your order email.

Changes and cancellations

If you need to change or cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible. We will confirm what is still possible based on its production status.

If there is a problem with your order

If your item arrives damaged, differs from what you ordered or there is a delivery problem, contact support with your order reference. Include a photo if it helps explain the issue.

The right to withdraw has an exception for items made to your specifications or clearly personalised. You retain any rights that apply when an item is defective or differs from what you ordered.
