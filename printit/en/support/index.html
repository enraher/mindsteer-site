Before you
get in touch.

A few answers to keep your idea moving.

Where are my designs saved?

Editable projects are saved on your device. Open them again in My designs. Copies sent to prepare a preview or order are managed separately.

How much is delivery?

The cost and estimated dates are calculated when you enter your shipping address, before you pay. Your order may be split into several shipments.

Can I cancel an order?

It depends on the order’s status and what the provider allows. Request cancellation as soon as possible from the order details if the option is available. A request does not guarantee cancellation.

What can I personalise with PrintIT?

Add photos, text, drawings and emojis to the products available in your catalogue. Personalisation options depend on the item.
