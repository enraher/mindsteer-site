This page describes how PrintIT is designed to handle data. The data controller is Enrique Ramírez Hernández. For privacy questions, contact print@mindsteer.app.

Account and designs

The app creates a guest identity with Firebase and stores your projects and preferences on your device. When you prepare a custom preview or an order, it may upload your print image to the service's private storage.

Orders and delivery

We use your country, name, address, email and phone number to quote and fulfil your order. Stripe processes the payment. Printful or Gelato receives the shipping details and file it needs. Resend uses your order email address to send your confirmation, tracking link and status updates.

When you pay, we store with the order a copy of the terms of sale you accepted: the date of acceptance, version, language, country and text. It is kept for as long as the order is kept, to handle claims and legal obligations, and is included in your confirmation email.

Cloudflare hosts the service and private print file storage. Firebase manages the guest identity.

Notifications and news

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging uses an installation identifier to notify you about order changes. You can manage each order's notifications in the app and control notification permission in your device settings.

If you opt in to news and offers, we save your email address and preference. You can opt out in Settings. Order emails are managed separately.

Temporary print copies

Preview copies expire after two hours. We remove files uploaded for a quote from 24 hours after their last upload, provided no active quote or order still needs them. We remove a paid order's print file once all shipments have been processed and the file is no longer needed for another purchase. Your editable projects stay on your device.

For books and other multi-page products, the app uploads the print PDF to the service's private storage. If the order is not paid, it is removed like other quote copies. If it is paid, it is kept for up to 30 days after it is sent for production, so the printer can download it and issues can be resolved, and then removed.

Access and deletion

You can request account and data deletion. Contact us at the same address to exercise other privacy rights.
