Practical support for everyday tasks

Getting started can be hard, even when you know what needs doing. Choose one task, write a first action and keep the next step in view. TaskSteer is designed for adults who want practical support with tasks and routines, including adults with ADHD.

  • Create tasks with small steps you can edit and mark done.
  • Choose a 2, 5, 10 or 25 minute session, or work without a timer.
  • Pause and resume. A session can finish while your task stays unfinished.
  • Explore 36 practical tools for starting, breaking tasks down, time and transitions, routines, returning and energy or overwhelm.
  • Adapt 36 templates for everyday tasks, from replying to an email to doing laundry.
  • Make space for a pause with Calm breathing, meditation and audio.

Your tasks and your account

Tasks are saved on your device. Sign in to sync saved task data with your TaskSteer account. TaskSteer and MindSteer have separate accounts, saved data and subscriptions. Using the same sign-in provider does not combine the two products.

TaskSteer Premium includes all guides, templates and task sessions. Free Calm content remains available. Prices, billing periods and any eligible trial are shown in the app before purchase.

Support when you need it
