Beauty Scan privacy policy

Updated November 28, 2026

Who this policy covers

Enrique Ramírez Hernández (Enrasoft), Spain, operates Beauty Scan for Android, package com.enrasoft.cameraugly.facedetector. This policy covers Android version 2.0.0 (13) and later versions that follow the same practices. Contact enrasoft@gmail.com for privacy questions, rights requests or questions about earlier versions.

Camera and results

The app asks for the Android camera permission when you choose to open the camera. It displays the live front or rear camera preview and temporarily holds a frame in memory to show a thumbnail on the result screen. The app does not save the image to disk, upload it, or share it. The thumbnail is discarded when you leave the result. The game generates scores randomly. It does not measure facial features or use face recognition, biometric identification or image analysis to produce them.

You can deny or revoke camera access in Android Settings. The camera workflow needs that permission.

Data stored on the device

The app stores your sound preference locally until you clear its data or uninstall it. Android may retain the current scores temporarily to restore the result screen. The app has no account, ads, analytics or remote crash reporter and does not send app data to Enrasoft. No in-app payments are offered.

Website, contact and rights

Opening this policy launches your browser. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may receive connection data such as your IP address under the GitHub privacy statement. This page has no analytics or advertising scripts.

If you email us, we use your address and message to answer your request and retain them as needed to address it and comply with applicable obligations. Avoid sending photos or other unnecessary personal information. Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, or contact your supervisory authority. In Spain, that authority is the AEPD.
